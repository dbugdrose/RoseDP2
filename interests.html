Interests


The Natural World

A core theme in Mary Oliver's work, nature was a key part of her poetry and her life.

Poetry

Mary Oliver referred to poetry as a "life-cherishing force" that is "necessary as bread."

Teaching

Oliver believed that writing should be accessible, and her teachings centered observation and gratitude.

Walks Through Nature

Mary Oliver took walks often, and her walks were the subject of poems such as "How I Go Into the Woods."

Literature

She appreciated classic authors such as Walt Whitman, John Keats, and Percy Bysshe Shelley.

Her Partner

Mary Oliver had a loving and devoted relationship to her partner, photographer Molly Malone Cook.
